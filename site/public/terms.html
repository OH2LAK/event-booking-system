2. The service

We host a separate instance of the open-source event booking system for your event at your-name.hamradio-events.com. You get your own database and your own setup and admin passwords. The software itself is free to run yourself under the MIT licence; you pay for hosting, not for the software.

3. Ordering and payment

An order is a request. We review it and may approve or reject it. If we approve it, we email you the price and a payment link (card payment through Stripe). Your instance is created automatically after payment. The fee is for one event and covers the hosting period until the event ends plus the retention period below. Prices are shown including or excluding VAT as stated in the offer.

Own domain (optional). For an extra fee your instance can also answer at a subdomain you control, for example ilmo.yourclub.fi. You must own or control the domain, add the DNS record we give you, and keep it in place while the event runs. We issue and renew the certificate. If the record is missing or wrong, the address does not work, but your instance stays available at your-name.hamradio-events.com. We remove the own-domain setup when the instance is erased; remove the DNS record from your side afterwards.

4. How long we keep your data

Your instance stays online until your event has ended. We then switch it off and permanently erase the database 30 days after the event ends. We email you before erasure. Export the reports you need before then. Erased data cannot be restored. You can ask us to erase earlier.

5. Your data and our role

You are the controller of the attendee data you collect; we are your processor and process it only to run the service. Applicable data protection law requires a data processing agreement; we provide one on request, and you should ask for it before collecting attendee data. You are responsible for informing your attendees (the booking system has a privacy notice you configure in Event setup) and for having a lawful basis for the data you collect.

Sub-processors we use to run the service: Hetzner (servers), Cloudflare (network, DNS and web delivery), Stripe (payments), and, if you enable SMS, Setera (SMS delivery). Email is sent through the mail provider we configure for the platform.

The service is for organising events such as meetings, camps and courses. Do not use it for anything unlawful, to send unsolicited messages, or to collect data you have no right to collect. We may suspend an instance that is abused or that endangers other users.
