Privacy notice

For visitors to hamradio-events.com and people who order hosting. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Who we are

This website and the hosting service are run by Finskas Networks Oy (Finland, Business ID 3291260-2), by Erik Finskas, radio amateur OH2LAK. Finskas Networks Oy is the controller of the data described in this notice, except for attendee data in hosted instances (see below).

This website

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Our delivery network (Cloudflare) processes technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

The order form

When you request hosting we collect your name, email address, organisation, event details, the address you want, and any notes you write. We use them to review and handle your order, to create your instance, to bill you, and to contact you about it. We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations; accounting records are kept as required by Finnish law. If your order is rejected, we delete the details after a reasonable time.

Payments are handled by Stripe; we do not see or store your card number. Our email and server providers process the data on our behalf.

Attendee data in hosted instances

For the data that attendees give to your event, you (the organiser) are the controller and we are the processor. See the terms and ask us for the data processing agreement. Attendees should contact the event organiser about their data.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction or erasure, and you can object to or restrict processing, as the law provides. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuojavaltuutettu).
